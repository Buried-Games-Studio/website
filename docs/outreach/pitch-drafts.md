## Read this before spending any time — two different jobs

These targets are **not equivalent**, and treating them as one list is how outreach
time gets wasted. Every pitch below does one of two jobs:

**LEADS — reaches people who commission games.** Directories, trade press, the
listicles, the featured-company notes. These can produce an actual enquiry.

**AUTHORITY — reaches players, not buyers.** Consumer gaming media. It will not
send you a single client. It is worth doing anyway *only* because the binding
constraint on this site is domain authority — the 05.08.2026 audit found **2
inbound links**, which is why 57 URLs sit unindexed and the commercial pages rank
on page two. A link from a real regional outlet lifts the whole domain's ability
to rank for the terms that do convert. That is the entire case for it.

**If outreach time is limited, do the LEADS column first.** The authority pitches
are cheap (one email each) but slow, indirect, and easy to over-invest in.

| Target | Job | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Featured-company notes (§A) | **Leads + authority** | Highest reply rate; peer relationships |
| Listicle inclusion (§D) | **Leads + authority** | These pages rank AND get quoted by AI assistants |
| GamesMEA (§C) | **Leads** | B2B trade — actual buyers read it |
| IGDA Saudi, GCON (§C) | **Leads** | Industry rooms; peers refer work |

| Arab Hardware (§B) | **Authority** | Technical readers, high-quality domain |

## A. The new hook — the market guides

We published two buyer's guides on 05.08.2026 that name real companies:

- `/guides/game-development-companies-kuwait` — Duwaween, Sooqista, Diwaniya Labs,
  Vertex, Buried Games, Juego
- `/guides/game-development-companies-saudi-arabia` — Steer, Starvania, Shafrah,
  Athr, Buried Games, Juego

**Tell every company they are in it.** This is the highest-reply-rate outreach we
have, because it is not a favour request — it is a courtesy, and it invites a
correction rather than a link. Some will share it, some will link, some will ask
for an edit, and the edit request is itself the start of a relationship.

> **Subject:** You're in our guide to game studios in [Kuwait / Saudi Arabia]
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> We published a guide to game development companies in [market] and included
> [Company] in it: [URL]
>
> It's not ranked and nobody paid to be in it — we're a studio too and we're in
> the list, which we say plainly on the page. I've described [Company] as
> [one-line summary as written], based on your site.
>
> If anything there is out of date or you'd word it differently, tell me and I'll
> change it.
>
> Fahed Elahmad · Buried Games Studio · buriedgames.com

**Do not** ask for a link in this email. If it comes, it comes.

## D. Listicle inclusion (#62)

These pages already rank for the queries we want and get quoted by AI assistants.
Being added to one is worth more than most links.

**Targets:** Juego's "Top 5 Game Development Companies in Bahrain" (we are absent
despite /game-development-bahrain being our best-performing country page) ·
Capermint's "Top 21 Game Dev Companies in GCC" and their Saudi list · Pairroxz ·
TekRevol.

Juego publishes its inclusion criteria — technical breadth, production maturity,
engine specialisation, scalability, geographic positioning, transparency. Answer
them in order; that is what the email is for.

> **Subject:** Addition for your [list name]

>

> Studio isn't on it and I think we meet the criteria you publish:
>

>   Nabsh (nabsh.fun), Power of Bombs; Arrab in development
> · **Specialisation** — Arabic-first and RTL-native builds, not localisation
>   added at the end
> · **Leadership** — founder Fahed Elahmad; Creative Director Bokhari Hamid
> · **Registered entity** — Buried Games OÜ, registry 17564681
>
> Studio page: buriedgames.com · press kit: buriedgames.com/press
>
> Happy to supply anything else you need to verify.
